THE QUICK ANSWERS

Good questions.

Here are a few things people often ask us.

How long does delivery take?

We usually dispatch within 1–2 business days. Most orders arrive within 3–7 business days, depending on where you are.

Can I return or exchange something?

Of course. You have 14 days from delivery to request a return or exchange, as long as the item is unworn and in its original condition.

How do I choose the right size?

Our pieces are designed for an easy, comfortable fit. If you’re between sizes or need a hand, send us a note and we’ll help you choose.

Is checkout safe?

This client demo simulates checkout locally. It does not collect payment details, process payments or send real orders.

How can I reach the Common Thread team?

Use our contact form Monday through Saturday. We’ll get back to you soon.
